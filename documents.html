The documents you may need depend on the service

Requirements can vary according to the document type, destination country, receiving authority and purpose. Use these categories as a practical starting point.

01
Personal Documents

Affidavits, declarations, identity-related documents and personal undertakings.

02
Educational

Degree certificates, mark sheets and other academic documents for overseas use.

03
Commercial

Company documents, agreements, invoices and business-related paperwork.

04
Legal Documents

Power of attorney, declarations and other documents requiring formal execution.

BEFORE YOUR APPOINTMENT

Bring a complete document set

Having the correct originals, copies and identity documents ready can help avoid delays during review and processing.

Important

Do not assume every document follows the same process. If the document is intended for another country, confirm the destination requirements before submitting it.

General checklist
Original document or the document version requested for processing
Valid government-issued photo identification
Photocopies where the receiving authority requires them
Supporting documents connected to the purpose of the document
Passport or destination details when overseas use is involved
Previous attestation/apostille documents, if applicable
SERVICE-SPECIFIC

What to prepare for each service

These are indicative requirements for the four core services offered on this template.

Affidavit & NotarizationDraft or completed document, valid identity proof, supporting evidence where relevant, and any required witnesses or photographs.

AVOID DELAYS

Before you submit

Small document errors can cause rework. Check the basics before handing over your documents.

  • Make sure names, dates and identification details are consistent.
  • Check that pages are complete and signatures are placed correctly.
  • Keep originals safe and provide copies only when requested.
  • Tell the team if the document is for use outside India.
  • Ask whether the receiving authority has any special format or validity requirements.
