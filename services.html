What We Handle

Service 01

Affidavit & Notarization

Support for affidavits, declarations, copies and other documents that require notarization.

  • Preparation of affidavits and sworn declarations.
  • Certified true copies of original documents.
  • Signature and identity verification guidance.
  • Advice on which format the destination requires.

Service 02

Document Apostille

Guidance for documents that need apostille processing for use in participating countries.

  • Review of documents for apostille eligibility.
  • Guidance on the correct authentication route.
  • Support for Hague Convention destinations.
  • Clear updates on turnaround and fees.

Service 03

Embassy Attestation

Structured assistance for document attestation requirements connected to overseas use.

  • Guidance on non-Hague destination requirements.
  • Advice on the order of authorities involved.
  • Document checking before submission.
  • Coordination support through the sequence.

Preparation guidance and document support for powers of attorney and related formalities.
