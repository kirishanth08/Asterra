SERVICE OVERVIEW

A clear process for documents that need notarization

Notarization is often required when a document needs a formal notarial act to support the authenticity of a signature, declaration or execution. The exact requirement depends on the document and the organization receiving it.

Our role is to make the process easier: we help you identify what needs to be completed, check that the document is ready for the appointment, explain the next step and coordinate the notarization process.

DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

What should you bring?

Requirements can vary by document. As a general preparation checklist, keep the following ready.

Original documentBring the document that will be signed or notarized.
Valid identity proofCarry an accepted government-issued identity document.
Supporting documentsKeep any papers referred to in the affidavit or declaration.
Receiving-party instructionsBring any checklist or wording requirements provided by the organization.
Before you sign
  • Do not sign the document beforehand if the notarial procedure requires signing in the notary's presence.
  • Check that your name and identification details match your identity proof.
  • Confirm whether witnesses, photographs or additional supporting documents are required.
  • If the document will be used outside India, confirm whether apostille or embassy attestation is also required.
